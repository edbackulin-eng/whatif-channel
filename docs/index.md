# Hypothetica

**Hypothetica** is a YouTube channel about scientific "what if" scenarios: what
would really happen if the Moon's orbit decayed, if the Earth stopped turning,
if the internet went away. The premises are impossible. The physics around them
is not, and that is the point of the channel.

Every video states plainly when a premise could not actually occur, and says so
early rather than pretending otherwise. Where the underlying science is
genuinely disputed, the video says that too instead of picking the more
dramatic side.

## The channel

[Hypothetica on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Hypothetica)

## How the videos are made

The videos are produced by an automated pipeline. It is a private tool, run by
the channel owner for this one channel. It is not a product and not offered to
anyone else.

- Scripts are written with AI assistance and fact-checked against named sources
  before recording. Every figure used is recorded with the source it came from.
- Narration uses a synthetic voice. It does not imitate any real person.
- Imagery is public-domain archive photography from NASA and ESA, images
  generated with an openly licensed model, and diagrams drawn by the pipeline.
- Music is licensed for commercial use and credited in every description.

Videos containing AI-generated imagery or synthetic narration are uploaded with
YouTube's synthetic-media disclosure set, and the description says so in plain
words.
